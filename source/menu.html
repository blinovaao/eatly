Frequently asked questions

  • How long does delivery take?

    Delivery usually takes 30-60 minutes, depending on distance and demand.

  • How does it work?

    Food delivery works by you placing an order through an app or website, where a restaurant prepares your food. A delivery driver then picks it up and brings it to your location. You can track the delivery in real time.

  • How does your food delivery service work?

    Our food delivery service is simple and quick. Just place an order on our website or app, and our team will prepare it fresh. Once ready, our delivery partner will bring it right to your door, with tracking available so you know when it’ll arrive. Enjoy your meal!

  • What payment options do you accept?

    We accept a variety of payment options for your convenience, including credit and debit cards, mobile wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay, and, in some locations, cash on delivery. Let us know if you need any help choosing the best option!

  • Do you offer discounts or promotions?

    Yes, we regularly offer discounts and promotions! Check our website or app for the latest deals, and subscribe to our newsletter to stay updated on special offers.
